EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Essential details regarding attendance eligibility, speaker curation, ticketing timeline, and the December 12 edition at Tunis Business School.

TEDx is a grassroots initiative created in the spirit of TED's overall mission to research and discover "ideas worth spreading." TEDxTBS is an independently organized event operating under official license from TED, led entirely by student leaders and executive directors at Tunis Business School.

TEDxTBS welcomes passionate university students, ambitious entrepreneurs, academic faculty, researchers, industry executives, and creative thinkers. Seat allocations are strictly curated to maintain an intellectually vibrant, diverse audience.

Speaker nominations and open calls are managed directly by the Curation & Speaker Coordination division. Applications remain open through late October 2026. Use the nomination button in the speakers section or register below to submit candidates.

Priority badge registration unlocks in waves. Joining the priority dispatch list below guarantees first-in-line access 48 hours before general ticketing opens to the public.

If you hold an official invitation code (e.g. VIP, Student Innovator, CS50 Guest), head directly to the Ticket Pass section, enter your code to validate your credentials, type your name for live embossing, and download your high-resolution official PNG pass instantly 100% free.
